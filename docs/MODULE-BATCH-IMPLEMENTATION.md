## Implemented scope

| Area | User workflow | Controls and integration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equipment (`/equipment`) | Register inventory; issue equipment; acknowledge or dispute receipt; request a return; inspect and accept/return the request; record administrator write-offs. | Version conflicts, custody history, due dates, employee self-service, scoped HR access, configurable inspection and exit-clearance rules. Open custody can block offboarding and direct termination. |
| Handbook (`/handbook`) | Draft and publish immutable editions, assign a specific edition, open/read it, acknowledge it as the employee, supersede or withdraw an assignment. | Body/version snapshots, required acknowledgements, deadlines, history, private assignment scope, administrator policy. Required outstanding acknowledgements block onboarding completion. |
| Employment (`/employment`) | Propose a transfer or employment-field change; independently review it; apply it explicitly on/after its effective date; review service-period corrections and continuity. | Current employee-version checks, reporting-manager validation, service history, configurable approval/continuity rules. No automatic salary rewriting. Lifecycle completion and rehire preserve the service ledger. |
| Retention (`/retention`) | Configure periods by document type or default; view eligible documents; place employee/document preservation holds; request archive or restoration; independently decide the request. | No enabled policy is assumed. Holds, pending renewals, open lifecycle evidence and current qualification evidence prevent archive. Changed employee/document/policy state requires a fresh request. Downloads keep existing access; archived documents cannot be renewed or used as new checklist/certificate evidence until restored. Files are retained, not permanently deleted. |
| Helpdesk (`/helpdesk`) | Configure working windows, holidays, timezone, reminders and automatic escalation; view processing state/history. | New cases save their calendar and routing snapshot. In-app reminders are deduplicated. Escalation requires an eligible saved handler and preserves confidentiality. Automation is disabled until configured; an in-process worker runs every five minutes while the application is awake. |
| Recurring workforce | Edit/cancel selected future occurrences of a series through Manage recurring series. | Atomic occurrence/version checks, existing attendance/time restrictions and eligibility checks. Changed offers require fresh employee acceptance. Historical work is preserved. |
| Qualification evidence | Attach a private employee document to a renewal; resubmit if returned; independently verify it. | Pinned file/version/type/dates, current validity checks, private downloads and editable evidence rules. Retention cannot archive evidence supporting pending renewals or active qualifications. External certificate verification is still a provider integration. |
| Performance (`/performance`) | Assign up to 200 participants in one operation; choose duplicate handling; withdraw or reinstate a participant. | Atomic batch, cycle/version checks, independent assigned reviewers, history preservation. Withdrawn participants cannot submit or change objectives and are excluded from active cycle/report counts. Published content is preserved. |
| Leave (`/leave`, rules in `/hr-rules`) | Request a full day or office half-day; proceed through up to four distinct approval stages; view decision history. | Administrator-configured eligibility and ordered reviewers, saved calendar/rules, 0.5-day balance units, versioned decisions. Pending requests reserve balance; only final approval debits it. Office halves use the saved schedule midpoint. Complementary halves can coexist; overlapping periods cannot. Worked time and roster conflicts use the saved partial-day window. |
| Operational reminders (`/reminder-rules`) | Administrators configure reminder periods and enablement for approval queues, document expiry, learning, handbook acknowledgement and equipment return. | In-app delivery only, delivery deduplication, active recipient checks and archived-document exclusion. No external messages are sent by implementing these features. |
| Internal induction (`/learning`) | Company-branded course authoring and publication; private text/PDF/image/video lessons; employee learning player; timed, server-scored quizzes; individual/bulk assignment; attempts, completion review, certificates and scoped reports/CSV. | Immutable releases, hidden answer keys, employee-only attempts, configured pass/retry/deadline rules, private media budget, administrator branding/history and independent exemptions. Required training is assigned on onboarding and blocks completion while outstanding. No paid LMS or new dependency. |

The four new operational pages and reminder administration are wired into application routing and navigation. Existing workflow pages expose the remaining additions. Administrator/superadministrator controls govern configurable policies; the server retains row-level access and independent approval requirements.

## Configuration and practical limits

- Management office defaults remain Sunday–Thursday, 09:00–17:00, Asia/Qatar. A company or employee rule can override the calendar. Half-day leave applies to one office workdate; shift-based employees continue using whole roster days.
- Business rules affect the workflows described above. Saved requests retain the applicable snapshot. Enabling new policies does not silently rewrite historical decisions, pay or ledger balances.
- Free hosting remains Render Free and Supabase Free. Helpdesk processing runs every five minutes and operational reminders every fifteen minutes while the application is awake; the saved repetition rule prevents repeated delivery. Sleeping instances process reminders after waking. No paid scheduler or hosting resource is added.
- Retention provides preservation and reversible archive/restoration. Permanent file erasure and statutory retention schedules are not implemented or presumed approved.
- EOS remains an external integration pending its repository/API contract. Resend needs the owned sender domain/configuration. WhatsApp, biometric/offline hardware, external credential/LMS validation and bank/statutory payroll adapters remain separate integrations.
- No real employee records, rules, notifications, credentials or production files were created or changed by this implementation batch.
