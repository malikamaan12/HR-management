Attendance & Time Tracking Feature Prompt
This document details the requirements for the Attendance & Time Tracking module, outlining features, data fields, workflows, and Qatar-specific considerations.
I. Core Features
Multi-Method Attendance Tracking: Support for various methods including QR Codes, Biometrics, Mobile App Check-in, and Manual Entry.
Geofencing: Define virtual boundaries around work locations (especially event sites) to automate attendance tracking.
Real-Time Attendance Monitoring: Dashboards for managers to view employee attendance in real-time.
Shift Scheduling: Create and manage employee work schedules.
Overtime Calculation: Automated calculation of overtime based on Qatar Labor Law.
Leave Integration: Seamless integration with the Leave Management module.
Reporting & Analytics: Generate reports on attendance patterns, overtime hours, and labor costs.
Mobile App Integration: Allow employees to clock in/out and view their schedules via a mobile app.
Qatar Compliance: Adherence to Qatar Labor Law regarding working hours, overtime, and breaks.

III. Workflows
Clock-In/Clock-Out:
Employee uses QR code, biometric device, or mobile app to clock in/out.
System records timestamp and location.
Geofencing verifies location if enabled.


Shift Scheduling:
Manager creates shift schedules.
System notifies employees of their assigned shifts.
Employees can view their schedules via the mobile app.


Manual Entry:
Manager manually enters attendance for employees who did not use automated methods.
Requires approval from a designated authority.


Overtime Calculation:
System automatically calculates overtime hours based on Qatar Labor Law (25% for regular overtime, 50% for weekend work).
Overtime requires manager approval.


Attendance Exception Handling:
System flags attendance exceptions (late arrivals, early departures, absences).
Manager investigates and resolves exceptions.


IV. Mobile App Features
Clock-In/Clock-Out: QR code scanning, GPS-based check-in.
Shift Schedule Viewing: Display assigned shifts.
Time Sheet Submission: Submit timesheets for approval.
Leave Request: Submit leave requests.
Notifications: Receive notifications about shift changes, attendance exceptions, and other important updates.
Offline Functionality: Allow clock-in/out even without internet connectivity (data syncs when connection is restored).
V. Reporting & Analytics
Attendance Summary Report: Total hours worked, absences, late arrivals, early departures.
Overtime Report: Total overtime hours, overtime costs.
Labor Cost Report: Total labor costs by department, location, or employee.
Attendance Trend Report: Identify patterns in attendance data.
Geofence Report: Track attendance within geofenced areas.
VI. Qatar-Specific Considerations
Working Hours: Adhere to the maximum working hours allowed by Qatar Labor Law (typically 8 hours per day, 48 hours per week).
Break Times: Ensure employees are provided with required break times.
Overtime Regulations: Accurately calculate overtime pay based on Qatar Labor Law.
Rest Days: Ensure employees receive their required rest days.
Record Keeping: Maintain accurate attendance records for at least one year as required by Qatar law.
WPS Integration: Ensure accurate attendance data is used for payroll processing and WPS compliance.
VII. Integration Points
Employee Database: Retrieve employee information and update attendance records.
Payroll Management: Transfer attendance data for payroll processing.
Leave Management: Integrate with leave requests to adjust attendance records.
HRIS System: Share attendance data with other HR systems.
Geofencing Services: Integrate with geolocation services to define and monitor geofences.
Biometric Device APIs: Connect to biometric devices for attendance tracking.
This detailed feature prompt provides a comprehensive foundation for building a robust and compliant Attendance & Time Tracking module. Prioritize accuracy, automation, and user experience to ensure efficient attendance management and compliance with Qatar Labor Law.
