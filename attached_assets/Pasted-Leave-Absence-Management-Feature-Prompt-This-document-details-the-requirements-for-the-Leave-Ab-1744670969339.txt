Leave & Absence Management Feature Prompt
This document details the requirements for the Leave & Absence Management module, outlining features, data fields, workflows, and Qatar-specific considerations.
I. Core Features
Leave Request Submission: Employees can submit leave requests through a self-service portal or mobile app.
Automated Workflow: Requests are routed to the appropriate approvers based on pre-defined rules.
Leave Accrual: Automatic calculation of leave accruals based on employment type, tenure, and Qatar Labor Law.
Leave Balance Tracking: Real-time tracking of available leave balances for each employee.
Conflict Checking: System prevents overlapping leave requests within the same team or department.
Integration with Attendance: Automatic updates to attendance records upon leave approval.
Reporting & Analytics: Generate reports on leave trends, utilization rates, and outstanding requests.
Qatar Compliance: Adherence to Qatar Labor Law regarding annual leave, sick leave, and other types of leave.
Mobile App Integration: Allow employees to submit and track leave requests via the mobile app.

III. Workflows
Leave Request Submission: Employee submits a leave request through the self-service portal or mobile app.
Approval Workflow:
Request is routed to the employee’s direct manager.
Manager approves or rejects the request.
If required, the request is routed to a second-level approver (e.g., Department Head).
Employee receives notification of the approval/rejection status.


Leave Accrual:
System automatically calculates leave accruals based on the configured accrual method.
Accrued leave is added to the employee’s leave balance.
Accrual is triggered on a predefined schedule (e.g., monthly, quarterly).


Leave Cancellation: Employee can cancel a pending leave request.
IV. Reporting & Analytics
Leave Summary Report: Total leave taken by department, employee, or leave type.
Leave Balance Report: Current leave balances for all employees.
Leave Request Report: List of all leave requests, including status and approval history.
Leave Trend Report: Identify patterns in leave requests over time.
Absenteeism Report: Track employee absenteeism rates.
V. Qatar-Specific Considerations
Annual Leave: Minimum 21 days of annual leave after one year of service (Qatar Labor Law).
Sick Leave: 15 days of sick leave per year (2 weeks full pay, 4 weeks half pay, beyond that unpaid). Requires medical certificate.
Maternity Leave: 50 days of maternity leave (Qatar Labor Law).
Hajj Leave: Entitlement to leave for Hajj pilgrimage (Qatar Labor Law).
Ramadan Working Hours: Adjust leave accrual and scheduling to accommodate reduced working hours during Ramadan.
Documentation: Ensure proper documentation is collected for sick leave and other types of leave.
Arabic Language Support: All forms and reports should be available in Arabic.
VI. Integration Points
Employee Database: Retrieve employee information and update leave balances.
Attendance & Time Tracking: Automatic updates to attendance records upon leave approval.
Payroll Management: Integrate with payroll to deduct leave days from salary.
Mobile App: Allow employees to submit and track leave requests.
HRIS System: Share leave data with other HR systems.
VII. Security Considerations
Access Control: Implement role-based access control to restrict access to sensitive leave data.
Data Encryption: Encrypt leave data at rest and in transit.
Audit Trail: Maintain a detailed audit trail of all leave requests and approvals.
This detailed feature prompt provides a comprehensive foundation for building a robust and compliant Leave & Absence Management module. Prioritize accuracy, automation, and adherence to Qatar Labor Law to ensure efficient leave management and employee satisfaction.
