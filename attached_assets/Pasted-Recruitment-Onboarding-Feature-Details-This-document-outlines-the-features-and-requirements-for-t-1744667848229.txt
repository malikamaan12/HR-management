Recruitment & Onboarding Feature Details
This document outlines the features and requirements for the Recruitment and Onboarding modules, detailing functionality, data fields, and integration points.
I. Recruitment Module
A. Core Features:
Job Requisition Management: Streamlined process for creating, reviewing, and approving job requisitions.
Applicant Tracking System (ATS): Manage applications, track candidate progress, and communicate with applicants.
Multi-Channel Job Posting: Post jobs to internal career sites, external job boards, and social media.
Candidate Screening & Shortlisting: Automated screening based on keywords and qualifications.
Interview Scheduling: Automated scheduling with interviewers and candidates.
Collaboration Tools: Enable interviewers to share feedback and collaborate on candidate evaluations.
Reporting & Analytics: Track key recruitment metrics (time-to-fill, cost-per-hire, source of hire).
Qatar Compliance: Ensure adherence to Qatar labor laws regarding job postings and candidate selection.

C. Workflow:
Job Requisition Creation: Department Head initiates, HR reviews & approves.
Job Posting: System posts to configured job boards.
Application Submission: Candidates apply through portal or job boards.
Automated Screening: System filters based on keywords and qualifications.
Shortlisting: HR reviews screened candidates and shortlists for interviews.
Interview Scheduling: System automates scheduling with interviewers.
Interview Process: Interviewers conduct interviews and provide feedback.
Candidate Evaluation: HR reviews feedback and makes a hiring decision.
Offer Management: HR generates and sends offer letter.
II. Onboarding Module
A. Core Features:
Automated Onboarding Workflows: Guided workflows for new hires, tailored to role and employment type.
Digital Document Collection: Securely collect required documents from new hires.
Electronic Signature: Enable electronic signing of employment contracts and other documents.
Task Management: Assign and track onboarding tasks for HR, IT, and the new hire.
Compliance Checks: Ensure all required compliance checks are completed.
Welcome Package: Provide new hires with essential information about the company and their role.
Integration with Employee Database: Seamlessly transfer new hire data to the Employee Database.
Qatar Compliance: Ensure onboarding process adheres to Qatar labor laws.

D. Workflow:
Offer Acceptance: Candidate accepts offer through the system.
Onboarding Initiation: System automatically triggers onboarding workflow.
Document Collection: New hire uploads required documents.
Task Assignment: System assigns tasks to HR, IT, and the new hire.
Compliance Checks: HR verifies compliance with Qatar labor laws.
System Access Provisioning: IT creates system access for the new hire.
Orientation & Training: New hire completes orientation and training.
Onboarding Completion: HR marks onboarding as complete.
III. Integration Points:
Employee Database: Seamless transfer of new hire data.
Recruitment Module: Trigger onboarding workflow upon offer acceptance.
Document Management System: Store and manage onboarding documents.
Digital Signature Service: Enable electronic signing of documents.
Qatar Ministry of Labor API: Submit employment contracts and other required documents.
Background Check Services: Integrate with background check providers.
IT Systems: Automate system access provisioning.
IV. Qatar-Specific Considerations:
Arabic Language Support: All forms and communications should be available in Arabic.
QID & Visa Verification: Ensure accurate QID and visa information is collected and verified.
Labor Contract Compliance: Ensure employment contracts comply with Qatar labor laws.
Document Attestation: Verify the authenticity of educational and professional certificates.
Medical Examination: Ensure new hires undergo a medical examination as required by Qatar law.
This detailed feature outline provides a solid foundation for building a robust and compliant Recruitment and Onboarding system. Remember to prioritize user experience and automation to streamline the process and improve the new hire experience.
